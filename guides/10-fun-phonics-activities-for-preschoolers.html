10 Fun Phonics Activities for Preschoolers (Simple & Low-Prep)

Updated September 24, 2026 • By GoPhonics Literacy Specialists • 7 min read

Preschoolers learn best when their hands and bodies are moving. Traditional worksheets and drills often lead to boredom. Here are 10 easy, engaging phonics activities you can play at home to teach letter sounds and early blending!

1. Bubble Pop Letter Sounds 🫧

How to play: Blow bubbles outdoors or use an interactive bubble popping app. Call out a phonetic sound (e.g. "/b/ as in bubble!"). Your child must pop bubbles while repeating the target sound.

2. Sensory Sand Tracing 🏖️

How to play: Pour salt or sand onto a tray. Call out a sound (/t/) and have your preschooler trace the letter with their index finger while saying the sound out loud.

3. Feed the Dinosaur Monster 🦖

How to play: Draw a dinosaur face on a tissue box. Write letter sounds on plastic bottle caps. Have your child "feed" the dinosaur caps that match the target sound you announce.

4. Alphabet Hopscotch 👣

How to play: Draw a hopscotch grid with sidewalk chalk and write a letter sound in each square. As your child hops into a square, they shout the corresponding phonetic sound.

5. Shaving Cream Sound Waves ☁️

How to play: Spread shaving cream on a baking tray. Let your child smooth it out and write lowercase letters while practice saying phonemes.

6. Sticky Note Treasure Hunt 🔎

How to play: Stick notes with single letters around the room. Give your child a flashlight and challenge them to find the letter that makes the sound /m/!

7. Playdough Letter Stamps 🎨

How to play: Roll out playdough and press letter stamps or cookie cutters into it. Talk about the sounds each shape makes.

8. CVC Toy Car Blending 🚗

How to play: Write 3 letters on paper (e.g. c - a - t). Have your child drive a toy car slowly across the letters while saying the sounds slowly, then speed up!

9. Phonics Fly Swat 🪰

How to play: Write letter sounds on index cards scattered on a table. Give your child a clean fly swatter. Call out a sound and let them gently swat the matching letter!

10. Storybook Sound Search 📖

How to play: During bedtime reading, choose one target letter sound (e.g. /s/). Have your child raise their hand every time they hear a word starting with that sound.
