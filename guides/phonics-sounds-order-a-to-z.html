The Best Order to Teach Phonics Sounds to Early Readers

Updated August 30, 2026 • 6 min read

When teaching toddlers and preschoolers to read, teaching letters in traditional alphabetical order (A, B, C, D...) can actually delay reading fluency. Educational research shows that Synthetic Phonics introduces high-frequency consonants and vowels first so children can start blending simple words immediately.

Phase 1 Sounds: S, A, T, P, I, N

With just these 6 letters, a child can decode dozens of words: sat, pat, tap, pin, tin, pit, pan, sit!

Phase 2 Sounds: C, K, E, H, R, M, D

Introduces secondary blends to unlock words like cat, hen, red, mad, kid, cup.
