What Are CVC Words? Definition, Examples & Teaching Tips

Updated September 24, 2026 • By GoPhonics Literacy Specialists • 5 min read

If you're helping a preschooler or kindergartener learn to read, you've likely heard teachers talk about CVC words. But what exactly are CVC words, and why are they considered the foundational building block of early literacy?

Definition of CVC Words

CVC stands for Consonant-Vowel-Consonant. A CVC word is a three-letter word made up of an initial consonant sound, a middle short vowel sound, and a final consonant sound.

Formula: Consonant + Short Vowel (a, e, i, o, u) + Consonant
Example: /c/ + /a/ + /t/ = cat

Why Are CVC Words So Important?

CVC words are 100% phonetically regular—meaning each letter makes its standard, predictable sound. Because there are no silent letters or tricky vowel teams, children build confidence by successfully blending individual sounds into real words independently.
