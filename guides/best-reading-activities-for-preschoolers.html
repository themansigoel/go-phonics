Best Reading Activities for Preschoolers: Fun, Engaging & Play-Based

Updated September 24, 2026 • By GoPhonics Literacy Specialists • 6 min read

Before children ever read full sentences, they need rich experiences with language, books, and phonetic sounds. The best reading activities for preschoolers don't feel like schoolwork—they feel like play!

1. Interactive Tap-to-Read Sentences 📖

How it works: Provide interactive decodable sentences where pressing a word highlights it in 3D, speaks the word out loud, and triggers animated scenes (like jumping frogs or rolling cars). This connects spoken speech to written text effortlessly.

2. Dialogic Picture Book Reading 🗣️

Don't just read books to your child—turn storytime into a conversation!

  • Ask open-ended questions: "Why do you think the dinosaur looks surprised?"
  • Predict what happens next: "What do you think is inside the egg?"
  • Point out environmental print: Point to street signs, cereal boxes, or storefront logos.

3. Sight Word Floating Bubble Pop 🎈

High-frequency sight words (like the, is, and, to, you) account for 50%+ of printed English text. Play floating bubble popping games where children pop target sight words floating across the screen to earn gold coins and stickers.

4. Rhyming Basket Sorting 🧺

Collect household objects (e.g. a small cat toy, a hat, a cup, a rug). Place two baskets labeled with rhyming targets and have your preschooler sort the objects into their matching rhyme families.

5. Build a Cozy Reading Nook ⛺

Create a special space with soft pillows, a warm lamp, and low bookshelves so books are easily accessible. When books are part of a warm, comforting environment, children naturally choose reading over passive video consumption.
